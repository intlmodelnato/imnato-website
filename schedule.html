Schedule

IMNATO 2027 takes place February 11–14 at the Washington Plaza Hotel in Washington, D.C.

Pre-conference study and briefings at the embassies of NATO member states are part of the program. Each delegation is briefed at the embassy of the country it represents. All other events take place at the Washington Plaza Hotel.
